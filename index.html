Built for 1v1

1v1 up to 5v5

One on one is the headline, with 2v2, 3v3 and 4v4 on the half court and 5v5 in the arena. Build both lineups, check ball at the top, first to 11 by ones and twos (or 21), win by two, clear it after a stop. Make-it-take-it is a toggle.

Handles that matter

Crossovers, double crosses, between the legs, behind the back, spins, hesis, in-and-outs, step-backs and snatch-backs. Catch a defender leaning the wrong way and you break his ankles.

Skill shooting

Hold and release. Hit the green and it goes in, every time. Slightly early or late can still drop if you're open, but never when you're contested. Layups and close shots are more forgiving.

Five AI levels

Rookie to Hall of Fame. Harder opponents react faster, read your moves better and miss less. They play by the same rules as you, with no cheating.

How to play

  1. Click Play in your browser, then press any key on the title screen.
  2. Choose One on One, pick your player, your opponent and a difficulty, then hit start.
  3. Win the check, attack your defender's lean, and release your jumper at the top of the meter.

Works on school Chromebooks and laptops with nothing to install. Your settings and key bindings are saved in the browser.
